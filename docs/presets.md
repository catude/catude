# Presets: work profiles based on public setups

Profiles → `p` opens a gallery of ready-made work profiles, each **based on what a well-known practitioner has
published** about how they work with coding agents: their process, the tools they use, and the skills, agents
and commands they have released. `catude presets` lists them; `catude profile load preset:ID` installs one.

> These presets are catude's reading of public material. They are **not affiliated with, written by or
> endorsed by** the people they credit. Every preset links its sources so you can read the original.

## What a preset contains

| | |
|---|---|
| Philosophy and process | A short paraphrase and the steps of their workflow, with links to the posts, talks and repositories |
| Tools | The command-line tools they use; ✓ marks the ones installed on your machine |
| Skills, agents, commands | The files they published, each pinned to a commit of their repository, with its license |
| Plugins and MCP servers | Named, with the official command when the author documents one |
| Settings | The model and effort they say they use: shown, never applied |
| Not verified | Whatever the research could not confirm, stated openly |

Included today: Armin Ronacher, Boris Cherny, Dex Horthy, Geoffrey Huntley, Harper Reed, IndyDevDan,
Jesse Vincent, Kieran Klaassen, Lauren Tan, Matt Pocock, Mitchell Hashimoto, Peter Steinberger,
Simon Willison, Steve Yegge and Thorsten Ball. Several of them mainly use other harnesses (Cursor, Pi, Codex,
Amp); their presets say so and carry over what applies to Claude Code.

## What loading a preset does

Press `enter` twice on a preset (or run `catude profile load preset:ID`). catude then:

1. Downloads **only** files that are pinned to a commit **and** openly licensed (MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD…).
   Files without a license are all rights reserved: they are listed with a link, never copied.
2. Verifies, extracts safely and audits them exactly like any other profile ([details](profiles.md)).
3. Saves a work profile named after the preset. Nothing in `~/.claude` changes.

It never installs hooks, never changes your model or effort, and never installs plugins: when the author
ships a full plugin (often with hooks that their workflow relies on), the official install command is shown
and the choice is yours. Then `v` on the profile asks Claude Code itself whether every item loads.

## The image in the corner

Each preset shows a monogram of the person's initials. catude does not draw people's faces: turning a photo
of someone into ASCII art and attaching it to a product would use their likeness without their consent.
If you publish your own [Rigcard](rigcard.md), you can add your own art in `owner.art` (up to 12 lines of 40
columns) and catude shows it.

## How presets are made, and how to fix one

`internal/presets/presets.json` is generated by `scripts/build-presets.mjs` from the research files in
`research/`. Before a release every artifact is checked against GitHub (it must exist at its pinned commit)
and every preset is loaded and verified with a real Claude Code. `go test ./internal/presets` checks that
each preset cites https sources and produces a valid card.

If a preset misrepresents you or your work, or you would rather not be listed, open an issue and it will be
corrected or removed. Corrections with a source link are welcome from anyone.
